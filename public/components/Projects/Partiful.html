Iterative Redesign

The following page displays our iterative work in a collaborative project with Partiful. Our goal was to address Partiful's concern of their event creation process being too formal for simple hangouts. We designed a new workflow for casual events that is swift and simple.

Sketching & Wireframe

Introduction & Context

From our sketches, we decided to incorporate elements that streamlined the user experience. The main focus became the New Event and Hangout interface. We simplified Hangout creation using a calendar-adjacent layout and reduced the page to essential inputs. We also highlighted features like Party Genie and Calendar Sync to improve discoverability.

Sketches

Wireframes

We translated our sketches into wireframes with bigger buttons, cleaner layouts, and simplified elements aligned with Partiful's playful aesthetic.

Wireframe Feedback

Our team received several useful comments from our intermediary and Partiful contact:

  • When2meet integration felt too formal.
  • The calendar on the homepage looked too crowded.
  • The homepage had too many visual elements.
  • Being labeled a "host" felt too formal for casual events.

In response, we focused on simplifying the homepage, minimizing unnecessary elements, shrinking Party Genie, and refining the calendar.

Hi-Fi Prototypes

After iterating on the wireframes, we built high-fidelity prototypes in Figma to reflect a streamlined, casual interface. We emphasized direct interaction with upcoming events and simplified calendar functionality.


Conclusion

This project allowed us to engage deeply with iterative design practices while collaborating with a real-world partner. From sketching and wireframing to building high-fidelity prototypes, each phase was informed by constant user feedback and design critiques.

Our redesign aimed to strike a balance between Partiful’s playful brand and the need for a lightweight flow tailored to casual hangouts. Through simplification of interfaces, rethinking hierarchy, and ensuring accessibility, we successfully delivered a more intuitive, engaging, and fun user experience.

Moving forward, we would like to conduct more user testing on elements like the hangout time picker and mobile responsiveness, while continuing to refine interactive feedback components like the RSVP icons.
