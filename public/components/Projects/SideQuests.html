Overview

Side quests is an app that allow users to break routine through the use of in real life challenges "side quests". These sidequests are then shared in a social media style feed where one can view friend's completed sidequests.

The Problem

The app was mainly develop to combat monotony and routine in the busy and packed life of college students. We aimed to have a fun and social way to take one's mind off studying and classes while also share fun experiences that one may have not thought of before.

Research

Inspiration

We were inspired by the growing popularity of gamification and social spontaneity in everyday life. Apps like Duolingo and Fitbit demonstrated how point systems and streaks could motivate consistent engagement, while platforms like BeReal showed how authentic, casual social sharing could create genuine connections among friends. Our goal was to combine these ideas: creating a lightweight, fun app where real-life experiences could be transformed into friendly competitions and shared memories.

Conclusion

Working on SideQuests was a rewarding experience that allowed us to combine user-centered design, gamification, and technical implementation into a cohesive product. From initial research and interviews to prototyping and high-fidelity design, we focused on addressing the needs of college students seeking a fun, low-effort way to break out of routine and reconnect with friends. We learned that simplicity, spontaneity, and social motivation are key drivers of engagement for our audience. Designing with these insights helped us create an app that transforms everyday life into playful challenges, fostering joy and connection through lightweight interactions. This project deepened our collaboration skills and reaffirmed the value of thoughtful design in solving real human problems.
