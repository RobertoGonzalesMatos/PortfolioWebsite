Iterative Redesign

The following page displays our iterative work in a collaborative project with Partiful. Our goal was to address Partiful's concern of their event creation process being too formal for simple hangouts. We designed a new workflow for casual events that is swift and simple.

Issue

The issue we were presented for this project was to expand Partiful's reach to catch hosts on the planning stage and allow them to create simple and casual hangouts. The current partiful workflow has too many steps and feels too structure. Once you have the invite, there is an activity and a time set in stone which may not always accomodate to all invitees.

Inspiration

To solve many of these issues we looked at apps that are normally used to plan hangouts. Most people use google calendar or when2meet. Both of these platforms allow the users to look at other's free times and allows people to plan accordingly giving options to choose when the hangout time could be.

Sketching & Wireframe

From the gathered info and inspiration we decided to bring our own ideas through sketches!

Sketches

From our sketches, we decided to incorporate elements that streamlined the user experience. The main focus became the New Event and Hangout interface. Notably, we wanted the focal point of the home page to be the New Event button and the New Hangout interfaces. We simplified Hangout creation using a calendar-adjacent layout and reduced the page to essential inputs. We also highlighted features like Party Genie and Calendar Sync to improve discoverability.

Wireframes

We translated our sketches into wireframes with bigger buttons, cleaner layouts, and simplified elements aligned with Partiful's playful aesthetic.

As we can see in the carousel, there were three versions of the home page and two versions of the workflow. This came from the different approaches we could have to the idea of hangouts. There were many tradeoffs between simplicity and options the users can have while creating their hangouts. We decided to make many versions to show our stakeholders.

Hi-Fi Prototypes

After iterating on the wireframes, we built high-fidelity prototypes in Figma to reflect a streamlined, casual interface. We emphasized direct interaction with upcoming events and simplified calendar functionality.

Though it was difficult we belive our current design is able to find a compromise between simplicity and options for the user just like partiful wanted. Additionally they loved our hi-fi models and the idea of having the time as a driving factor to create the hangouts rather than activities!


Conclusion

This project allowed us to engage deeply with iterative design practices while collaborating with a real-world partner. From sketching and wireframing to building high-fidelity prototypes, each phase was informed by constant user feedback and design critiques.

Our redesign aimed to strike a balance between Partiful’s playful brand and the need for a lightweight flow tailored to casual hangouts. Through simplification of interfaces, rethinking hierarchy, and ensuring accessibility, we successfully delivered a more intuitive, engaging, and fun user experience.

Moving forward, we would like to conduct more user testing on elements like the hangout time picker and mobile responsiveness, while continuing to refine interactive feedback components like the RSVP icons.
